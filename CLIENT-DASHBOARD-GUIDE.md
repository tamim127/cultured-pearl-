# Cultured Pearl — Client Wix Dashboard Guide

Welcome to your new **Cultured Pearl** website management portal! This guide explains step-by-step how you and your team can manage website content, add upcoming events, review volunteer applications, and handle donations directly from the official **Wix Dashboard** without needing to write any code.

---

## 🔑 Accessing Your Wix Dashboard

1. Open your web browser and go to:
   **[https://manage.wix.com/dashboard/6f7426b7-afd2-4e14-84db-49d2395b4ee7](https://manage.wix.com/dashboard/6f7426b7-afd2-4e14-84db-49d2395b4ee7)**
2. Sign in with your registered Cultured Pearl Wix administrator account.

---

## 1. Managing Leadership & Team Members

When new board members join or bios need updating:
1. In the Wix Dashboard left navigation menu, click **CMS** (Content Management System).
2. Click on the **TeamMembers** collection.
3. To edit an existing leader:
   - Click directly into any cell (`title`, `role`, `bio`, `quote`, `image`) and type your changes.
4. To add a new team member:
   - Click **+ New Item** at the top right.
   - Enter their Full Name (`title`), Role (`role`), and Bio.
   - Double-click the `image` cell to upload their portrait photo from your computer or Google Drive.
5. Changes save automatically and appear on the live `/meet-the-team` page.

---

## 2. Managing Events & Gatherings

To add a new Debutante Ball, Sisterhood Gala, or Community Service Day:
1. In the Wix Dashboard left navigation menu, click **Events** → **Upcoming Events**.
2. Click **+ Add Event**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Event Name:** e.g., *"2027 Annual Sisterhood Gathering"*
   - **Date and Time:** Select start date, start time, and time zone.
   - **Location:** Enter physical venue address or virtual link.
   - **Short Description:** Enter a 1-2 sentence overview.
   - **Event Image / Cover:** Upload a banner image.
4. Under **Registration / Tickets**, select **RSVP** or **Paid Tickets**.
5. Click **Publish Event**.
6. The event will appear immediately on the **Home Page**, **Events Catalog (`/events`)**, and have its own dedicated RSVP page.

---

## 3. Reviewing Contact & Volunteer Submissions

When a visitor submits a question, prayer request, or volunteer application:
1. In the Wix Dashboard left navigation, click **Contacts**.
2. Every person who fills out a form is automatically added to your contact list with their name, email, and phone number.
3. To view specific form submissions:
   - Click **Communications** → **Forms & Submissions**.
   - Select either **Contact Us** or **Volunteer & Stewardship Application**.
   - You will see a timestamped spreadsheet of all responses including volunteer skills and prayer requests.
   - You can export this list to Excel/CSV anytime.

---

## 4. Managing Testimonials & Community Voices

To update the quotes displayed on the Home Page:
1. In the Wix Dashboard, click **CMS** → **Testimonials**.
2. Click **+ New Item** or edit an existing row.
3. Fill in the person's name (`title`), their relationship (`role`), and their testimonial (`quote`).

---

## 5. Setting Up Live Payments (Credit Cards, Apple Pay)

Your donation portal and magazine store are pre-configured to accept payments. To connect your nonprofit bank account for live card processing:
1. In the Wix Dashboard, click **Settings** → **Accept Payments**.
2. Select **Wix Payments**, **Stripe**, or **PayPal**.
3. Follow the guided prompt to enter your organization's 501(c)(3) EIN and bank account details.
4. Once verified by Wix, all online gifts will deposit directly into your nonprofit bank account.
